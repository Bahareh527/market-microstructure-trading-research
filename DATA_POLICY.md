# Data policy

The committed sample is generated locally by this repository. It is synthetic and
does not represent any person, exchange, broker, employer, or trading account.

## Allowed

- Synthetic data generated by src/market_microstructure/synthetic.py
- Small public datasets with an explicit redistribution license and full attribution
- Locally generated experiment metrics and figures

## Not allowed

- Employer or client data, code, identifiers, file paths, or business rules
- Brokerage credentials, API keys, account numbers, or private endpoints
- Purchased or licensed market data without redistribution permission
- Raw notebook outputs that may expose local paths or sensitive values

Put any privately licensed data under data/private. That directory is ignored by Git.
